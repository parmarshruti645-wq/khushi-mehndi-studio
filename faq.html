Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our products and services.

🌿 About Our Products

Is your henna 100% natural and organic?
Yes, absolutely! All our henna products are made from 100% pure Lawsonia Inermis leaves sourced from certified organic farms in Sojat, Rajasthan. We never use PPD, chemical dyes, synthetic preservatives, or any adulterants. Every batch is lab-tested for purity before packaging.
What is the shelf life of your mehndi products?
Our dry henna powder has a shelf life of 24 months when stored in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight. Premixed cones last 6 months refrigerated (use within 2 weeks after opening). Aftercare balm lasts 18 months. All products are clearly labeled with expiry dates.
Is your mehndi safe for sensitive skin and children?
Yes! Our mehndi is dermatologist-tested and approved for sensitive skin. It is completely free from PPD (paraphenylenediamine) and other harsh chemicals. We recommend a patch test 24 hours before full application for anyone with known skin allergies. Our Kids' range uses an even gentler, extra-mild formula.
What is the difference between your henna cones and powder?
Our premixed mehndi cones are ready-to-use — just snip the tip and apply. They're perfect for beginners and quick applications. Our henna powder requires mixing with lemon juice, essential oils, and sugar to make a paste — it gives you more control over consistency and is preferred by professional artists for fresh, maximally potent paste.

🚚 Orders & Shipping

How long does delivery take?
Standard delivery takes 3-5 business days across India. Express delivery (1-2 days) is available for metro cities at an additional charge. International orders typically take 7-14 business days. You'll receive a tracking number via email/SMS as soon as your order is shipped.
Do you offer free shipping?
Yes! We offer free standard shipping on all orders above ₹499 within India. For orders below ₹499, a flat shipping fee of ₹49 applies. International shipping rates vary by country and are calculated at checkout.
Can I track my order?
Absolutely! Once your order is shipped, you'll receive an email and SMS with your AWB tracking number. You can track your package on our website or directly on the courier's website. You can also log into your account to see real-time order status.
What if I receive a damaged or wrong product?
We have a 100% satisfaction guarantee. If you receive a damaged, defective, or incorrect product, please contact us within 48 hours of delivery with a photo. We'll arrange a free replacement or full refund immediately — no questions asked.

✨ Mehndi Services

How far in advance should I book bridal mehndi?
We recommend booking bridal mehndi at least 3-6 months in advance for peak wedding season (October-February). For off-season dates, 1-2 months is usually sufficient. Popular dates fill up very quickly, so don't delay! A 30% advance payment secures your booking.
Do you provide home service?
Yes, we provide home service across Jaipur, Delhi, Mumbai, Ahmedabad, and many other major cities. Our artists carry all their own equipment. A small travel surcharge may apply for locations beyond 20km from our studio. Contact us to check availability in your area.
How long does the mehndi stain last?
With proper aftercare, our premium mehndi typically lasts 2-3 weeks on the hands and 1-2 weeks on the feet. The stain darkens over the first 48 hours after removal. Using our aftercare balm, avoiding soap on the design for 24 hours, and keeping the area moisturized significantly extends the color.
Can I request a specific design style?
Absolutely! Our artists are trained in Arabic, Rajasthani, Pakistani, Moroccan, Indo-Western fusion, and modern minimalist styles. You can share reference images at the time of booking. We also offer a free design consultation session for bridal packages to ensure you get exactly what you envision.

💡 Application & Aftercare Tips

How long should I leave mehndi on?
For best results, leave the mehndi paste on for 6-12 hours (or overnight). The longer you leave it, the darker the stain. Remove by gently scraping — never wash with water. After removal, apply our aftercare balm and avoid water contact for at least 12-24 hours.
Why is my mehndi stain light?
Light stains can result from: not leaving the paste long enough, washing too soon after removal, using water before the stain develops, or using old/expired henna. Tips for darker stain: leave paste overnight, use our aftercare balm immediately after removal, add essential oils to your paste, and apply after a shower (warm skin absorbs better).
Can I mix your henna powder with other brands?
We recommend using our henna exclusively for best results, as mixing with unknown brands can affect color and consistency. If mixing is necessary, ensure the other product is also 100% natural with no chemicals. We provide detailed mixing instructions and formulas in our blog and design books.
