Our Story

15 years of passion, tradition, and the finest henna artistry.

Established 2010

Born from a Love of Ancient Art

Mehndi Studio was born in the lanes of Jaipur's historic Johari Bazaar in 2010, when founder Meena Devi noticed that the traditional art of mehndi was being lost to cheaper, chemical-laden alternatives.

With a deep respect for her grandmother's recipes and a passion for organic purity, she began crafting small batches of pure henna using age-old techniques — triple-sifting, stone grinding, and sun drying.

Word spread. Brides flew in from across India. Today, Mehndi Studio has adorned over 5,000 brides, ships to 20+ countries, and remains proudly rooted in Rajasthani tradition while embracing modern elegance.

What Drives Us

Our Mission & Values

🌿

Organic Purity

We source only from certified organic farms in Sojat, Rajasthan. No chemicals, no PPD, no compromises. Your skin deserves nothing but the best of nature.

🎨

Artistic Excellence

We believe mehndi is living art. Our artists undergo years of training to master each style — from delicate Arabic florals to intricate Rajasthani patterns.

🏛️

Cultural Heritage

Mehndi is more than beauty — it's centuries of tradition, ritual, and celebration. We're proud custodians of this art form and committed to keeping it alive.
